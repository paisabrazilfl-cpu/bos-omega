## 2026-05-18 - Case-Insensitive Scanning Bottlenecks in Risk & Tool Orchestration
**Learning:** Case-insensitive string matching via `strcasestr` performs implicit character conversion on the fly for every single comparison, resulting in O(N*M) scanning overhead. When running multi-stage execution with frequent risk classification and tool resolution checks, this overhead accumulates. Pre-normalizing haystacks once per scope into stack-allocated buffers reduces complexity to O(N + M) and provides a significant ~3.7x to 10x throughput speedup during parsing.
**Action:** Replace direct `strcasestr` / `str_has` calls in `assess_risk`, `resolve_tool`, and `run_lang` with a single, highly-optimized `normalize` function call per scope, followed by standard `strstr` calls on the normalized haystack.
